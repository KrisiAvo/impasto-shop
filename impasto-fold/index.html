The mechanics

Why it feels different from a wallpaper trick

Every fold on screen is computed from the real angle of your lid, not a canned animation looping underneath it.

Reads the real hinge

IOKit talks directly to the internal lid-angle sensor Apple already ships — down to 0.01° on Apple Silicon Macs — so the fold always matches how far the lid actually is.

Rendered, not recorded

A single Metal fragment shader projects your live desktop through the closing angle in real time: perspective, blur and dimming, all on the GPU.

Knows where you're sitting

Eye height and keyboard tilt shape the perspective, so the fold still reads correctly on a stand, propped up, or on your lap.

Camera calibration, kept private

Point it at your face once and Impasto Fold reads eye position locally to fine-tune the view. Frames are never saved or sent anywhere.

Only when it means it

Closing past 90° starts the effect; sitting still doesn't. Open back past 90° and it's gone — no lingering, no lag.

Stays out of the way

The overlay is click-through and excludes its own window from the capture, so nothing about your workflow underneath changes.

Check before you install

What Impasto Fold needs from your Mac

Works on

  • macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later
  • Any Apple Silicon MacBook Air or Pro (M1–M4)
  • Intel MacBooks with the T2 chip, 2018–2020 (coarser 1° steps)

Won't work on

  • Pre-2018 Intel MacBooks — no internal hinge sensor
  • Desktop Macs — iMac, Mac mini, Mac Studio, Mac Pro
  • External monitors — the fold only applies to the built-in display
Also needs Screen Recording permission for the live mirror, and one right-click → Open the first time you launch it — Impasto Fold is ad-hoc signed, not notarized, so macOS will ask once and then leave it alone.
